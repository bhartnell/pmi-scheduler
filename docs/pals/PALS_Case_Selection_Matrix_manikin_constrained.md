# PALS 2025 — Case Selection Matrix (manikin-constrained, targeted selection)

**Selection policy (program, deliberate — documented rationale, not a shortcut):**
> Cases are selected — **not randomly drawn** — to (a) match available **manikin fidelity**,
> (b) target **documented local performance gaps** identified by the accrediting body and partner
> agencies, and (c) calibrate challenge level. AHA's random-draw model averages case difficulty
> across many cases; our selection is deliberate for a paramedic-scope, prehospital audience.

**Manikin inventory:** neonate (high-fidelity) · infant · toddler.
**NOT available:** school-age (~6–10 yr) · adolescent.

Legend: ✅ fits inventory · ⚠️ needs substitution/imagination · ❌ poor fit
