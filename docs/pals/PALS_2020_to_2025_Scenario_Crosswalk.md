## ✅ P13 / Tension Pneumothorax — CORRECTION (I was wrong)

I earlier flagged the "already intubated" premise as in-hospital framing to strip. **That was
wrong, and the 2025 manual settles it.**

**2025 Testing Case Scenario 11's own PREHOSPITAL lead-in reads:**
> *"Prehospital: You are **on scene** with an 8-year-old boy. He was **intubated** with an oral[tracheal tube]…"*

AHA itself puts this case **on scene, with the patient already intubated.** The intubated premise
isn't a hospital artifact — it **is the clinical mechanism**: over-aggressive PPV in a small chest
→ barotrauma → pneumothorax → tension, sustained by continued positive pressure. It's the flight/
transport failure mode, and DOPE exists precisely for the deteriorating intubated patient.

**Keep:** the intubated premise, DOPE, needle decompression.
**Reframe only:** the endpoint (chest x-ray + chest tube → transport / receiving facility).

*Note on the "don't teach medics to dart kids" objection: the case's design answer is that
decompression must be the **conclusion of an assessment** (lung sounds, compliance change, DOPE) —
which is exactly what the scenario forces. Skipping the case would produce the reflexive medic the
objection fears.*

## Prehospital / OOH reframing

**All 15 cards ship a native `Prehospital:` lead-in — none needs one invented.** Reframing is only
needed where the *management* leans on in-hospital resources.

**Needs reframing (ranked):**
1. **P13 — Tension Pneumothorax. Worst offender.** Even the *prehospital* lead-in presupposes the
   patient is **already orally intubated**, and the case resolves with **chest x-ray + chest tube**.
   Reframe: BVM/supraglottic instead of pre-placed ETT; keep needle decompression; endpoint =
   decompress + transfer. **Do not port as-is.**
2. **P7 — Lung Tissue Disease.** Ends in *"intubation and mechanical ventilation by an expert"* +
   PICU. Reframe endpoint to PPV/CPAP + rapid transport.
3. **P14 — Cardiogenic Shock.** Needs inotrope titration, cardiology consult, echo. Reframe to:
   recognize, cautious small bolus, stop if heart failure worsens, PPV support, transport.
4. **P8 / T7 — Septic Shock.** Hinge on **antibiotics + blood cultures within the hour** and
   vasoactives — **out of paramedic scope ⇒ N/A** (per the scope/N-A grading model). Keep fluids +
   reassessment as the graded actions.
5. **P2 — Hypovolemic / NAT.** Needs trauma-surgical consult + CT. Reframe to: recognize shock +
   ICP signs, mandatory-reporter duty, expedite transport.
6. **P4 — Upper Airway Obstruction.** Mostly in scope (nebulized epi). Gate or strip **heliox** and
   **dexamethasone** per protocol.

**Clean as-is for prehospital (minimal work):** P5, P9, P11, P12, P15, P16, T9, T13.

*Note: "standalone ER / urgent care" is the right reframe device for P13/P7/P14 — it grounds the
students without pretending a paramedic crew has a chest-tube tray or an echo.*
