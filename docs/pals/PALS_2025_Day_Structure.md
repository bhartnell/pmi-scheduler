# ⚠ READ FIRST — 3 corrections to the request

### 1. The Team-Leader requirement is TWICE, not once. ← build-affecting

The request says *"we're verifying **at least once each**."* **That is wrong.**
AHA 2025 (Module 6, Lesson 12) states verbatim:

> **"Every student must act as Team Leader — at least TWICE in the full PALS Course."**

**And the math has zero slack:** 6-student groups × 12 practice cases = **exactly 12 TL turns = 2 per
student.** **Any group larger than 6 breaks the AHA requirement** unless cases are added.

⇒ The hub's TL tracker must count **≥2 per student** and **warn when a group exceeds 6**.

*(Separate and easy to confuse: for **testing**, AHA allows a student to satisfy the requirement
"as Team Leader **or a team member**." So testing pass must NOT require 2 TL runs. The ≥2 TL rule
lives in **practice**.)*

### 2. Lesson 5 is new in 2025 — 60 min of mandatory BLS skills testing
2020 had **no Lesson 5**. 2025 adds **5A Child BLS Test (30 min)** + **5B Infant BLS Test (30 min)**,
both with checklists. **Program decision:** covered by our ACLS/BLS block (class splits adult vs
infant/peds), records carried into the PALS file via the skills export. **Hold a 30-min contingency.**
*(Also new: Lesson 11 — Coping With Death, 20 min, optional. We already run the ACLS version.)*

### 3. Learning stations: **3** in 2025, not 4 — and only **ONE** rhythm station
2020 ran 4 stations (with 2 rhythm stations). 2025 describes **3 core stations, 20 min each**.
Instructor ratio is specified: **6:1, max 8:1.**

# ⚠ TIME BUDGET — the 2-day course only fits because we carry BLS testing

AHA 2025's own durations total ≈ **790 min** of instruction (355 core + 300 practice + 75 testing +
60 exam), before breaks and lunch. Two days of 08:30–17:00 gives ≈ **840 net min** after lunch and
breaks.

**That is ~50 minutes of slack.** Add Lesson 5's 60 min and **it does not fit.**
⇒ Carrying BLS testing from the ACLS block isn't a convenience — **it's what makes the 2-day course
fit at all.**

# 3. LAB SECTIONS → STATIONS

## LAB SECTION A — Learning Stations (Day 1, 14:45, 60 min)
**3 stations, 20 min each. Ratio 6:1 (max 8:1). NOT graded in the app** — attestation + printable
competency sheet into the student's file.

| Station | Lesson | What's practiced | Equipment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Airway Management** | 7C | High- vs low-flow O₂ (NC max 4 L/min); head tilt–chin lift + jaw thrust; titrate FiO₂ to SpO₂ 94–99% post-ROSC; **OPA** indications/sizing/insertion; suction ≤10 s; mask sizing; **E-C clamp bag-mask ventilation** | 2–3 pediatric intubatable airway manikins + intubation equipment |
| **Vascular Access / IO** | 8C | **Discuss indications AND contraindications** for IO, **and demonstrate** IO insertion; 3-way stopcock; rapid IV/IO bolus | IO-capable infant manikins + IO drills |
| **Rhythm Disturbances / Electrical Therapy** | 9C | ECG leads, monitor + rhythm strip, pad placement, **defibrillation 2–4 J/kg**, **synchronized cardioversion 0.5–1 J/kg**, rhythm recognition. Students demonstrate **one at a time** | ALS Baby + rhythm generator, or high-fidelity manikin |

⚠ **2025 has only ONE rhythm station** (2020 ran two). Running two is a program choice for throughput,
not an AHA structure.

## LAB SECTIONS B / C / D — Case Scenario Practice
- **4 stations**, students rotate, **NEW TEAM LEADER each rotation**.
- **25 min per case**, structured: **5 prebrief → 10 simulation (uninterrupted, stopwatch, no chairs) → 10 debrief** (Gather–Analyze–Summarize).
- Roles (6-student rotation): Team Leader · Airway · IV/IO/Meds · Monitor/Defib/CPR Coach · Compressor · Timer/Recorder.
- **Graded in app:** formative checklist (mirrors the testing checklist for that patho) + 0–4 TL rubric. **No PASS/NR.**
- **AHA requires 12 practice cases: 4 Respiratory, 4 Shock, 4 Arrhythmia.**

## LAB SECTION E — Case Scenario TESTING (Day 2, 13:10, 75 min)
- **4 stations × 3 SYNCHRONIZED ROUNDS**, 25 min per round. **All 4 stations run the SAME case in a
  round**; students rotate between rounds. One patho category per round.
- **ONE graded Team Leader per station per round**; all other students recorded as **team members** on
  the attempt (this is what satisfies AHA's "as TL *or a team member*").
- **PASS 2 of 3.** Result vocabulary **PASS / NR** (not Pass/Fail). Pass = **all IN-SCOPE steps checked**
  (out-of-scope steps = **N/A**, not a blank box).
- **Retest:** one immediate retest if time permits; otherwise the end-of-course remediation lesson.
  Retest the **entire** scenario. **Remediation + retesting within 30 days.**

⚠ **AHA 2025 prescribes RANDOM DRAW** from card pools (Test 1 = 4 cardiac cards; Test 2 = 8
respiratory/shock cards; two stations in parallel). **The program deliberately uses TARGETED
SELECTION** — documented rationale: match available manikin fidelity (no school-age/adolescent
manikin), target documented local performance gaps, calibrate challenge. **The hub should support
instructor-selected cases**, not force a draw.

**Test integrity verified: NONE of the 12 practice cases is a testing case.** Where a practice case
shares a pathology with a test case, it is a **different age band** — which is the same design
principle AHA's own bank uses (forces transfer of the algorithm rather than rehearsal of a memorized
case):

| Pathology | TEST card | PRACTICE card |
|---|---|---|
| Distributive/Septic | `TEST_CASE_07` — **4-mo infant** | `PRACTICE_08` — **12-yr adolescent** |
| Disordered Control | `TEST_CASE_13` — **4-yr child** | `PRACTICE_15` — **6-mo infant** |
| SVT | `TEST_CASE_09` — **3-mo infant** | *(not rehearsed — arrhythmia practice uses asystole / pVT / wide-complex / brady)* |

### ⚠ Two things the instructor must know on the day

**1. `PALS_PRACTICE_15` is a PARTIAL card — but it IS runnable.**
Present: lead-in, vitals, overview, objectives, PAT, and the **full primary ABCDE**. That is everything
needed to *run* the scenario. **Missing** (book pp.174–176 absent from our scan): the post-primary
intervene list and the debriefing tool. **Debrief from the DCoB checklist** plus the overview line:
*"discuss indications for intubation and methods to estimate appropriate endotracheal tube sizes."*
Re-scan pp.142–143 and 174–176 when book access allows.

**2. Manikin/age.** `PRACTICE_03` (10 yr), `PRACTICE_08` (12 yr), `PRACTICE_13` (8 yr) exceed the
manikin inventory (neonate / infant / toddler). Per program policy: **age-adjust with age-appropriate
vitals, or run on the largest manikin available and brief the age explicitly.** Both are acceptable;
instructor's call on the day.

### Confirmed, not an indexing miss
- The **testing bank is 14 cards**, not 16 (pages 137+ are the Team Dynamics Debriefing Tool and ECG
  rhythm strips).
- **Only TWO Disordered-Control-of-Breathing cards exist in all of AHA 2025**: `PRACTICE_15` (infant)
  and `TEST_CASE_13` (child). There is no third.

# 6. Other AHA-2025 requirements the hub must respect

- **Precourse self-assessment is a hard door check:** ≥70% **and a printed certificate**.
  *"Required: Collect the students' precourse self-assessment certificate."*
- **Exam:** ≥84%, open-resource, **online via Atlas** (paper contingency if wifi is unreliable).
- **No hints during testing:** *"without any assistance, hints, or prompting."* The **only** sanctioned
  prompts are the **scripted prompts printed on the checklist** — the hub must display those verbatim
  and nothing more.
- **Critical actions must be briefed up front** (e.g. failure to confirm airway placement, shocking a
  perfusing rhythm) — these force a no-pass.
- **CPR Coach is a formal role** (groups of 3–4; every student rotates through it).
- **Equipment note (AHA):** *"a jump kit and defibrillator unit for prehospital professionals."*
