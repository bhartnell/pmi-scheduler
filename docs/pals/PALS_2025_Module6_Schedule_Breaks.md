# PALS 2025 — Module 6 (Lesson Plans) sweep: what BREAKS the 2020 schedule

**Source:** AHA PALS 2025 Instructor Manual, Module 6 (Lesson Plans), all 64 pages OCR'd.
**Why:** Ben's warning — *provider content barely changes; class setup and testing change a lot,
and we found out afterwards.* Confirmed. **The 2020 schedule cannot be run unchanged in 2025.**

---

## 🔴 THE BIG ONE — Lesson 5 exists now, and it's 60 minutes you have not blocked

The 2020 schedule has **no Lesson 5**. In 2025 it exists and it is **mandatory BLS skills testing**:

- **5A — Testing Station: Child High-Quality BLS Test — 30 min**
- **5B — Testing Station: Infant High-Quality BLS Test — 30 min**
- *(5C — Child & Infant Choking — 20 min, **optional**, new)*

These are **testing stations with checklists**, not practice: *"Complete the Child CPR and AED
Skills Testing Checklist for each student."* Rotations are 10 min (5 min CPR + 5 min peer debrief),
groups of 3–4, one in-station retest allowed.

**Mitigation AHA offers:** run them in parallel — *"You may evaluate 2 groups simultaneously… 1 child
BLS and 1 infant BLS."* Even so it's a **30-minute hard add** plus a second manikin/instructor set.

**⚠ Also new: Lesson 11 — Coping With Death (optional, 20 min).** 2020 had no Lesson 11 either.

## 🔴 CORRECTION TO WHAT I TOLD YOU — the Team Leader requirement is real after all

I previously said (from Module 4) that AHA never requires a student to lead, because testing can be
passed *"as Team Leader **or a team member**."* **That is true for TESTING but I missed Module 6,
which imposes the requirement in PRACTICE:**

> **"Every student must act as Team Leader — at least twice in the full PALS Course."** (Lesson 12)

So the TL obligation lives in **practice**, not testing. **Your team-lead tracking is therefore
mandatory, not just a program nicety** — which vindicates your instinct to track it.

**And the math is brutal:** 6-student groups × 12 practice cases = **exactly 12 TL turns = 2 per
student. Zero slack.** Any group **larger than 6** breaks the AHA requirement unless you add cases.

## 🔴 Testing model changed — random draw from card pools, team-based pass

2025 Lesson 13 (Case Scenario Testing):
- **3 cases** for the full PALS Course. **"The team must pass 2 case scenarios."** *(Your pass-2-of-3
  matches. ✅)*
- Tested as a **team of 6**. *"A student may be tested as a team member if Team Leader is outside
  their scope."*
- **Scenarios are RANDOMLY DRAWN from physical card decks immediately before each test:**
  - **Test 1 pool (4 cards — cardiac):** SVT · Bradycardia · Asystole/PEA · VF/pVT
  - **Test 2 pool (8 cards):** Upper Airway Obstruction · Lower Airway Obstruction · Lung Tissue
    Disease · Disordered Control of Breathing · Hypovolemic · Obstructive · Distributive ·
    Cardiogenic Shock
- **Two testing stations run in PARALLEL** (6 students in test 1, 6 in test 2).

**⚠ This differs from the 2020 run** (*"all instructors have the same scenarios, in the order 7, 9,
13"*). 2025 = **random draw from pools**, not a fixed synchronized sequence. **Decision needed:** do
we adopt the AHA random-draw model, or keep the fixed synchronized-rounds model? Random draw means
the app must support a **draw-a-card** step and a **card-deck pool** per test.

## 🔴 Learning stations: 3 in 2025, not 4 — and only ONE rhythm station

2025 core stations, **20 min each (60 min total)**, *"Work with 6 students in this station"*:

- **7C Airway Management** (20 min) — high- vs low-flow O₂, NC max 4 L/min, head tilt–chin lift +
  jaw thrust, titrate FiO₂ to SpO₂ 94–99% post-ROSC, OPA indications/sizing/insertion, suction
  ≤10 s, mask sizing, E-C clamp BMV. Recorded on the PALS Course Progress Checklist.
- **8C Vascular Access** (20 min) — IO Access video, IO insertion, 3-way stopcock, rapid IV/IO bolus.
- **9C Rhythm Disturbances / Electrical Therapy** (20 min) — leads, monitor, pad placement, defib
  (2–4 J/kg), sync cardioversion (0.5–1 J/kg), rhythm recognition. **Students demonstrate one at a time.**

**Your 2020 build ran 4 stations including 2 rhythm stations. 2025 describes only ONE rhythm
station.** Keeping 2 is defensible (throughput), but it's your call, not AHA's structure.

**Instructor ratio is now specified: 6:1, max 8:1.**

## 🔴 Practice is now a prescribed COUNT and STRUCTURE, not a time block

- **Full PALS Course = 12 practice cases: 4 Respiratory, 4 Shock, 4 Arrhythmia.**
  *(Your 2020 blocks happen to total 12. ✅ But the category split is now mandated.)*
- **25 min per case**, structured: **5 min prebrief → 10 min simulation (uninterrupted, stopwatch,
  no chairs) → 10 min debrief** (Gather–Analyze–Summarize).
- 12 × 25 = **300 min (5 hours) of practice.**
- Roles for a 6-student rotation: Team Leader · Airway · IV/IO/Meds · Monitor/Defib/CPR Coach ·
  Compressor · Timer/Recorder.

## ⚠ Other hard gates (easy to miss, painful on class day)

- **Precourse self-assessment is a door check:** ≥70% **and a printed certificate**. *"Required:
  Collect the students' precourse self-assessment certificate."* Students must be warned weeks out.
- **Exam = 84%**, open-resource, **delivered online via Atlas.** If room connectivity is unreliable,
  you need a TC-approved paper contingency.
- **Test card decks must be physically prepared** (4 cards Test 1, 8 cards Test 2) — a setup task the
  2020 schedule never had.
- **Equipment note:** *"a jump kit and defibrillator unit for prehospital professionals"* — your
  audience.

## ⚠ AHA's own internal contradictions (not OCR errors — both read cleanly)

   → **Plan on 25.**
2. **Testing duration:** Lesson 13 header says **25 min/scenario**, but the Case Scenario Test 1 page
   says *"Take no longer than **8 minutes to test and 2 minutes** to give students feedback"* (=10 min).
   → Likely 25 = block allotment, 8+2 = active test window. The manual never reconciles them.

## Bottom line

**Module 6 contains no course agenda** — it points to the agendas in Module 5 / Part 2. Summing its
own durations for the full course: core lessons **415 min** + practice **300** + testing **75** +
exam **60** ≈ **850 min ≈ 14 h 10 m of instruction**, before breaks, lunch, optional lessons (+40),
and remediation.

That's **roughly an hour more instructional content than the 2020 build** — driven almost entirely by
**Lesson 5 (BLS testing, +60)** and **Lesson 4 (+20)**.

**Still a 2-day course, but the day plan must be rebuilt, not adjusted.**
