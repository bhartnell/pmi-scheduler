## What this is for

Two questions that came up on a lab day page: how to change the title shown under "Coverage - Shift Signups", and how to add a volunteer.

## The "Coverage - Shift Signups" box

- The words "Coverage - Shift Signups" are a fixed heading on the lab day page. There is no control on the lab day page to rename it.
- The lines listed inside the box are open shifts that fall on the same date as the lab day. Each line's title is the title of that shift.
- To change a shift's title, go to the shifts page (Scheduling, then Shifts), click the edit pencil on that shift, change the Title, and save. Only directors can edit shifts.
- Accept or decline a signup directly from the box on the lab day page.

## Adding a volunteer

- There is no button today to add a volunteer instructor to the availability list, for example when someone texts that they can help on a certain day. This is a known gap and a feature request is open.
- Current workaround: add them to the station from the station's Edit window using the "+ Custom name..." option. An email address is required.
- If the person has a login, an admin can set their role to Volunteer Instructor in Admin, then Users. They will then appear in the instructor list under "Volunteer Instructors".
- Volunteers who sign up through a volunteer event linked to the lab day show in the Volunteers section and in the blue Volunteering group of "Available Today".
