## What this is for

On a lab day page you may see a box headed "Coverage - Shift Signups". It lists the open coverage shifts for that lab day and who has signed up. This article covers two common questions: how to change the wording, and how to add a volunteer.

## Changing the title

There are two different pieces of text here, and they are edited in different places.

1. The box heading "Coverage - Shift Signups" is fixed. It is the same on every lab day and there is no control to edit it.
2. The title of each shift listed inside the box (for example, the topic covered) can be edited. Go to **Scheduling > Shifts**, find the shift, and use its edit option to change the title, description, date, and times. Only directors and admins can edit shifts. If you are an instructor and need a title changed, ask a director or admin.

Note: this changes the shift only. It does not change the lab day title or the station names on the lab day.

## Adding a volunteer

There is no "add volunteer" button today. Coverage shifts are filled when an instructor signs up for the shift themselves, and a lead instructor then accepts or declines the signup from the Coverage box.

If someone volunteers by text or in person (for example, for a specific date):

1. Ask them to sign up for the shift through the site, then accept the signup in the Coverage box on the lab day page.
2. If they need to be on a specific station, use the station's **Instructors** dropdown. Volunteer instructors have their own group in that list. The "+ Custom name" choice also works, but it requires an email address.

Adding volunteer instructors directly to the availability list is a known gap and is tracked as a feature request. This article will be updated when it is built.

## If something looks wrong

Use the feedback button on the page and include which lab day you were on.
